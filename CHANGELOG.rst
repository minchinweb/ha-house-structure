Changelog
=========

.. release:: unreleased

  - :feature:`-` The **Thermal Lab** page in the sidebar, moved here from Home Thermal Lab so that other integrations can share it: a tab bar and a frame for each tab's page. Integrations add tabs through ``hass.data["house_structure_tabs"]`` and the ``house_structure_tabs_updated`` event, and the page follows without a reload. House Structure's own tab, **House**, draws the house (or one room) and lists the equipment and detached buildings. Tab pages load ``tab.js`` (Home Assistant's connection and theme) and the vendored **Chart.js** from ``/house_structure_static/``.
  - :feature:`-` **Furnace**, **air conditioner** and **detached building** parts, moved from Home Thermal Lab: the furnace's input per stage, AFUE and blower curve, the air conditioner's size and sensible capacity, and a building's rooms. Published as ``equipment`` and ``buildings`` next to the rooms (the summary stays at version 1: only keys were added).
  - :feature:`-` **Repairs for a missing size are raised only when something needs that size**: an integration lists what it uses in ``hass.data["house_structure_needs"]`` and fires ``house_structure_needs_updated``. The repair names it. Before, every wall or roof without a size raised one, though House Structure's own sensors don't use it.
  - :feature:`-` **Doors**: a new part, placed in an outside wall or an internal wall, with its size and its **contact sensor**. It takes its room from the wall; a door in an internal wall joins the rooms on both sides. A door with a contact gets an **open** sensor that follows it, with the wall and the rooms on each side in its attributes. Published as each room's ``doors`` (a door in an internal wall in both rooms, each naming the other as ``other_area_id``), and walls and internal walls give their doors' area as ``door_m2``. A wall's ``net_m2`` attribute now leaves out its doors as well as its windows. The door's sensor also gives each side's temperature sensor (``temperature_entity`` and ``other_temperature_entity``). The **room and house drawings** show doors: on an outside wall in a row with its windows, standing on the floor; in an internal wall on the face the two rooms share, found from their positions (listed under the drawing when it cannot be placed). For Home Thermal Lab's door-state hypothesis (09).
  - :feature:`-` A **default basement wall depth below ground** on the house form, used by every below-ground room that does not give its own; the room form shows the house's value next to its own field. Published as the room's ``below_grade_m``, with ``below_grade_default`` saying it is the house's.
  - :feature:`-` The room summary also publishes each room's **width and depth**, and each wall's **face** in the house's grid and its typed **length and height**, so other integrations can size a room or wall that has no area.
  - :feature:`-` **Drawings** of the house, in the forms: each **room** as an isometric box (its footprint and ceiling height, its outside walls and windows, a face with no wall part dashed), and the **house** from its rooms at their positions and floor levels. Rooms take a **width, depth and position** (east and north of the house's south-west corner, and their floor's height); a room left without a position is drawn in a row.
  - :feature:`-` The **heating runs** on the house drawing: a register's **outlet** is typed from its room's corner (and height), the **plenum** from the house form, and each run is drawn from the plenum to its outlet, with its length beside the duct's stated length as a check. The runs are schematic: they do not follow the joists or walls.
  - :feature:`-` Windows keep their **glass width and height** for the drawings; the drawings are served at ``/api/house_structure/room.svg`` and ``/api/house_structure/plan.svg``, like the outline (not authenticated, as an image in a form cannot send the login token).
  - :feature:`-` A **repair for a missing size** names the wall or roof's **room** and links to its device, and the room to its area.
  - :feature:`-` Sizes can be typed **with their own unit**, whatever the house's: ``3.2 m``, ``12' 6"``, ``150 sq ft``, ``40 m³``. A bare number is still in the house's unit.
  - :feature:`-` A room lists the **rooms beside it and above it**; each link counts both ways, and the first edit ticks the other rooms on the same floor. Published as each room's ``neighbours`` (beside, above or below), on the room's sensor and in the diagnostics, without adding entities.
  - :feature:`-` Adding, changing or removing a part **no longer reloads the integration**, so the other parts' sensors stay available (no more blips of "unavailable" in their history). Changing the house's own settings still reloads it.
  - :feature:`-` A failed Open-Meteo fetch **keeps the last answer** while it still covers the time, instead of making every sensor unavailable for 15 minutes.
  - :feature:`-` **Diagnostics** download: the house and each part's settings and what was built from them, skipped parts, every area with its parts (so a room left out shows up), the sky now, and the OpenStreetMap outline as wall bearings and lengths (no position).
  - :feature:`-` **Feet and inches** as a unit: lengths are typed as ``12' 6"`` (or ``12 6``, ``150"``); floor areas and volumes in square and cubic feet.
  - :feature:`-` A **roof can cover several rooms**: it is listed in each room's summary (with ``area_ids``), its device sits in the first, and a skylight in it can name the room it lights. Roofs saved with one room keep it.
  - :feature:`-` The house form measures the **house rotation** from the building outline on OpenStreetMap and draws it, with **Redraw the map** to check another value before saving (from Home Thermal Lab).
  - :feature:`-` **Sky source** on the house: Open-Meteo (the default) or a solar radiation sensor, read every 5 minutes and split into the sun's beam and the rest of the sky (Erbs model).
  - :feature:`-` The sun's position comes from ``astral`` (as Home Assistant's ``sun.sun`` does), allowing for refraction near the horizon.
  - :feature:`-` Rooms, outside walls, roofs, windows, internal walls and heating registers, each a sub-entry of the integration and a device in a Home Assistant area. Sensors: sun on each wall and roof (W/m², from Open-Meteo every 15 minutes), solar gain through each window and each room's windows (W, following the blind's position), the temperature difference across each internal wall (from the areas' temperature sensors), and whether each register is heating. Sizes are typed in the house's unit and kept in metres. Each room's parts are published in ``hass.data["house_structure"]`` for other integrations, with a ``house_structure_updated`` event when they change. Split out of `ha-home-thermal-lab <https://github.com/minchinweb/ha-home-thermal-lab>`_.
